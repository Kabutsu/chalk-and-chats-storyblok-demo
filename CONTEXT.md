# Chalk & Chats

A local climbing community in Southampton (~150 people in its WhatsApp group), mostly indoor climbing with occasional outdoor trips. The website replaces an existing Wix site and is for the community's public face and day-to-day member needs.

## Language

**Chalk & Chats**:
The community itself. Written with an ampersand in copy, titles and navigation; the logo keeps its spelled-out "Chalk and Chats" lockup.
_Avoid_: Chalk and Chats (in copy), the club, the group

**Member**:
A person who takes part in the community, typically someone in the WhatsApp group.
_Avoid_: User, customer

**Newcomer**:
A visitor deciding whether to join the community.
_Avoid_: Prospect, lead, guest

**Admin**:
A non-technical volunteer who maintains the site's pages and copy and runs the community's events and competitions.
_Avoid_: Editor, moderator

## Events

**Event**:
Anything on the Chalk & Chats calendar. Every Event has a type: Session, Social, Competition or Trip.
_Avoid_: Meetup, activity

**Session**:
A regular climbing night at a Wall. Currently every Wednesday at The Climbing Hangar, and every third Monday at a second Wall.
_Avoid_: Meet, climb night

**Social**:
A non-climbing Event, such as a board games night or yoga.
_Avoid_: Meetup, hangout

**Trip**:
An occasional outdoor climbing Event.
_Avoid_: Outing, excursion

**Wall**:
An indoor climbing venue where Sessions and Competitions are held.
_Avoid_: Gym, centre, venue

## Merch

**Merch**:
Branded Chalk & Chats clothing (shirts, hoodies, tank tops), made to order in bulk and offered in a few colours and sizes (S to XL).
_Avoid_: Products, store items, swag

**Drop**:
A limited window during which Members can order Merch; orders are batched into one bulk order when it closes, then collected at a Session.
_Avoid_: Sale, pre-order, campaign

**Interest tally**:
An anonymous count of how many people would buy each Merch item and size, shown while no Drop is open so Admins can judge when a Drop is worthwhile. Stores no personal data.
_Avoid_: Waiting list, wishlist, pre-order

## Competitions

**Competition**:
A single-night indoor bouldering Event run by Chalk & Chats, where Competitors log the Problems they climb.
_Avoid_: Comp, contest, league, tournament

**Competitor**:
A person taking part in a Competition, identified only by the full name they self-report.
_Avoid_: Participant, player, climber (when meaning in-competition)

**Problem**:
A single bouldering route set for a Competition that Competitors attempt.
_Avoid_: Route, climb, boulder

**Zone**:
A scoring level short of the Top, such as getting off the ground or reaching halfway. Every Problem has the same Zones, and each Zone's points are set per Competition.
_Avoid_: Checkpoint, hold, partial

**Top**:
Completing a Problem; the highest scoring level.
_Avoid_: Send, finish, complete

**Category**:
The group a Competitor is ranked within: Men, Women or Open.
_Avoid_: Division, class, gender

**Result**:
One Competitor's self-reported outcome on one Problem: the highest Zone reached, up to a top.
_Avoid_: Score, entry, submission, log

**Leaderboard**:
The ranking of Competitors within a Category, calculated from their Results.
_Avoid_: Standings, scoreboard
